# Erskine Advisory

**Independent owner's representation for significant private residences.**

---

## What we do

We act for private owners and family offices, representing their interests on
significant residential construction projects. We review the contract before it
is signed, verify claimed work against work actually in place before payments are
released, administer change orders and the owner's statutory obligations, and
report in a form the owner can act on. We are paid by the owner and by nobody else.

## The one sentence, if it is useful

*"They check that the work exists before your client pays for it — and they keep
the holdback the Builders Lien Act requires, so your client isn't left funding
their contractor's unpaid trades out of money they have already paid."*

## From the architect's side

**What we absorb.** Client management and the decision calendar. Payment
application review against work in place. Change order logs and cost reporting.
Owner questions that would otherwise arrive in your inbox at nine on a Sunday. On
British Columbia projects, the owner's obligations under the Builders Lien Act —
the 10% holdback (s.4(1)), the holdback account (s.5), and the 45-day filing and
55-day holdback periods (s.20, s.8) — together with the title search before
release, which is prudent practice rather than a statutory requirement.

**What we never touch.** Design authority. The aesthetic argument is yours, and we
do not relitigate it with your client. We hold no opinion in front of an owner
about a design decision that is properly the architect's.

**What the role is built to do.** Give the owner an independent reader, so that
questions arrive earlier and decisions move forward rather than sideways. Put the
numbers through a check before they are contested rather than after. And give an
owner who needs to be told something they do not want to hear somebody other than
you to hear it from.

## Independence, condensed

- We accept compensation from no one but our client. No referral fees, finder's
  fees, rebates or commissions — not disclosed-and-accepted, none.
- We hold no interest in any construction company. We do not build and we do not
  contract for construction work; the owner signs the construction contract
  directly with their licensed general contractor.
- No percentage of savings, and no contingency arrangement tied to outcomes.
- Specialists we retain — engineers, cost consultants, inspectors — are paid by us
  on the client's behalf, and have no relationship with the contractor.
- We will say when an owner does not need us, even though it costs us the fee.
- Everything is confidential by default. No client name, project or image is
  published without written permission.

The full commitment is published and dated at **erskineadvisory.com/independence**.
