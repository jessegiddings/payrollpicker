## The problem it solves

A significant residential project runs two to three years, involves dozens of firms, and generates thousands of transactions and decisions. Every professional on it is paid by someone — and none of them holds, as their actual job, the owner's whole position: cost, schedule, quality and risk, together, on the owner's side only.

Full representation puts one party at the table with exactly that job, from design development through warranty and final reconciliation. We perform no construction work and hold no contract with the builder — you sign with your general contractor directly. Our role is to advise, review, verify, coordinate and report, continuously, on your side of that line.

## The standard scope

Everything the discipline is expected to cover, we cover:

- **Due diligence** on budget, contract, schedule and team before signatures — the [Pre-Construction Audit](/services/pre-construction-audit), credited against the full engagement
- **Permits and approvals** tracked and coordinated with the consultant team
- **Contract procurement**: bid comparison, negotiation support with your counsel, scope responsibility mapping
- **Budgeting** held against a real baseline, with independent cost consulting where the numbers warrant it
- **Team selection**: the consultant roster reviewed for gaps, references actually called
- **Scheduling** oversight against a baseline with logic behind its dates
- **Insurance and bonding** confirmed directly with brokers, not taken from the contractor
- **Closeout**: punch list to closure, warranties, as-builts, final reconciliation

## What we carry that most representatives don't

This is the part to compare against any other service list you are reading:

- **Payment application review against work in place** — every application, before approval
- **Independent draw verification and release approval** — in writing, or not at all
- **Statutory holdback administration** — [the obligation BC law places on you as owner](/services/holdback-and-draw-compliance), calculated on every draw and released to the statutory calendar
- **Change order interrogation and pricing challenge** — in scope or new, price supported or not, with a running ledger against contingency
- **Lien period tracking and title search** before any release
- **A documented decision log** — decisions made once, in writing, in a file that is yours to keep

## Who it's for

Owners committing to a multi-year project who want representation for its full arc — especially where the owner is remote, the site is complex, or the project's governance will be reviewed by a family office.

## Fee

A percentage of construction cost billed monthly with final reconciliation, stepping down as project size rises — or a monthly retainer for open-ended scopes. Quoted per engagement after we understand the project; the structure and our refusals are published on [the fees page](/fees).

## What it isn't

Not a second builder, and not a replacement for your architect or contractor — the project still needs both doing their jobs well ([the comparison page](/compare) sets out where each model belongs). Not a guarantee that nothing goes wrong; it is the structure that determines how early you find out and how strong your position is when something must be resolved.
